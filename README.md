# Stop loss, Tp

Polymarket BTC 5-minute momentum demo bot.

The demo bot polls a public CCXT BTC spot ticker every **500 ms** and compares the latest price with a sample about **1,000 ms earlier**. Its trigger adapts to volatility: it uses the **99th percentile of absolute one-second BTC moves** collected over a rolling window of up to **20 minutes**, with a **$1 minimum threshold**. After startup it waits for 120 valid move samples (about one minute at the default cadence); the baseline then grows to a full 20 minutes as data accumulates. The dashboard shows the current threshold and warm-up progress. Moves at or above the threshold target UP; moves at or below its negative target DOWN. Entries wait **3 seconds** after a five-minute market window opens. While a window is untraded, the latest signal remains active until an opposite signal. There is no best-ask price-band filter; each BUY targets **100 shares** and can sweep asks up to **50% above the observed best ask** by default (`MAX_BUY_SLIPPAGE_PERCENT`), subject to the **$0.99 ceiling**. The bot allows up to **five filled entries total per window**, with only one position open at a time; after an exit, a fresh signal can open the next position. It takes profit when the held-side best bid reaches **$0.75** and force-sells at **$0.30 below entry** when the entry price was below **$0.70**. Unexited shares remain subject to CLOB-threshold or official settlement.

Coinbase `BTC/USD` is the default feed: it is reachable from this runtime and its CCXT rate limit permits 500 ms polling. Set `CCXT_EXCHANGE` and, if needed, `CCXT_SYMBOL` to select another CCXT market; the feed honors an exchange's higher minimum interval if it requires one. Binance returned a location restriction from this runtime, so Binance connectivity is not assumed. The dashboard reports feed errors and staleness; a missing or stale feed cannot generate a signal.

Demo marketable orders use visible Polymarket book depth. BUYs are capped at the configured 50% slippage price and $0.99 ceiling; SELLs take profit at a $0.75 minimum price, while stop-loss SELLs are forced at the current visible bids. Thin books can produce partial fills, leaving the position open until the remaining shares sell or settle. Estimated crypto taker fees are included in demo cash and P&L. A held-side CLOB midpoint at or above **$0.99** is counted as a $1 payout per remaining share; a best bid at or below **$0.01** is counted as $0.00. This is a demo threshold heuristic, not official resolution. Shares still open at the five-minute close wait for official Polymarket resolution. Account equity is cash plus the marked value of all open shares; total P&L is equity minus starting capital, and marks remain available when the active market rolls over.

This strategy is strictly demo-only. `LIVE_TRADING=true` is refused before wallet authentication, and the active bot uses only `DemoTrader`. The $1,000 paper balance and history reset when the process restarts. Simulated fills, estimated fees, and P&L are behavior checks—not evidence of live execution or profitability.

Run `npm test` from this directory to verify the feed, adaptive threshold, order behavior, and live guard. For local startup and dashboard checks, follow `DEMO_RUNBOOK.md`.
