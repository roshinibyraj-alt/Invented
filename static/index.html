Event log
Scheduled SKIP windows do not trade or change stakes. If an UP or DOWN window fails the price trigger, its observed result still changes both next stakes; no position or P&L is created. A loss doubles the next demo ($100 base) and real ($1 base) stake; a win resets each to base. Stakes have no configured maximum; demo trading stops when the stake exceeds available demo capital. The existing real FAK ceiling is $0.99 per share, so an order triggered below $0.50 can fill higher. Real buys do not sell or redeem positions. Real USDC balance is available collateral, not held-share value.
The public Pause/Resume control stops queued and future real buys; an order already submitted may still fill. Demo trading continues and its results keep advancing the real stake ladder. The control resets to Resume-available after a service restart.
