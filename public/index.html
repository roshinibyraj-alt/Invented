Polymarket-only demo strategy

$1,000 shared demo capital

UP/DOWN share one cash pool. The $0.30 rung posts both sides; the first partial or full fill cancels the opposite order. The $0.70 rung posts just one order after one side signals.

Low rung · $0.30 · 10 shares

Post-only limits on both sides fill at exactly $0.30 only after a later public SELL print walks through the order. Low-rung losses increase its next size by 1.4×; it has no stop.

High rung · $0.70 signal → market buy

After a low-rung win, pause the low rung. As soon as one fresh side bid reaches $0.70+, sweep the currently visible CLOB asks immediately for 30 shares (100 on the one retry). The fill may be above $0.70; taker fees apply and no maker rebate is credited. A second loss returns to the low rung.

Take profit · $0.99 → $1.00 paper credit

Both rungs use a post-only TP and require a later public BUY print. Maker entry/TP fees are zero; modeled maker rebates are credited to paper cash and P&L.

High-rung stop · $0.30 best bid

Only the $0.70 rung has a stop. At a fresh bid of $0.30 or below, remaining shares are paper-sold at the observed bid and charged the modeled taker fee.

CLOB close-price paper outcome

At the final 3 seconds, a bid above $0.98 wins; otherwise the higher bid wins. Fallbacks keep paper positions from remaining pending. This is not official settlement.
