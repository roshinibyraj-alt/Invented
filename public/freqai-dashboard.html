Strategy details

BTC model signals with 100-share taker buys

FreqAI trains a LightGBM classifier on Binance 1-minute OHLCV. Labels are the direction of the enclosing 5-minute BTC candle at its close. Inputs use returns, candle shape, relative volume, volatility, and time within the window; Polymarket order-book pressure is not a model feature.

After a minimum of 45 seconds, require a fresh current-window probability strictly above 58%. Immediately request 100 shares at available asks. No price offset or Binance fallback. The unchanged model normally supplies its first usable current-window forecast after the first minute closes.

One executed entry per window. Sweep fresh visible asks from cheapest upward and show the actual average entry. If depth is insufficient, hold the partial fill and cancel the unfilled remainder immediately; nothing rests or rolls into another window. Opposite signals do not sell or add another entry.

Taker fees are included in cost and P&L: shares × market fee rate × price × (1 − price), calculated for each fill. BTC 5-minute markets default to a 0.05 fee rate. No maker rebates are credited. Paper execution cannot guarantee live fills.

Hold filled shares to official resolution, with no take-profit, stop-loss or recovery sizing. Freqtrade remains prediction-only in dry mode; no live orders are sent.
