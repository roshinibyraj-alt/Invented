Strategy details

BTC model signals with window-only recovery flips

FreqAI trains a LightGBM classifier on Binance 1-minute OHLCV. Labels are the direction of the enclosing 5-minute BTC candle at its close. Inputs use returns, candle shape, relative volume, volatility, and time within the window; Polymarket order-book pressure is not a model feature.

After the first minute can close, the bot chooses whichever class has the higher predicted probability. There is no minimum-confidence or edge gate, so a low-confidence forecast still selects a side. If no current-window model prediction exists by 80 seconds, a fresh Binance window-direction fallback is used and shown in activity.

Start each window with a 50-share paper buy. If the opposite side's fresh FreqAI probability is strictly above 60%, sell the held position against visible bids down to $0.01. Once sold, buy the opposite side, with at least 50 shares, sized so a $1 winning payout could recover this window's net realized losses and fees. No same-side adding.

Buys sweep visible asks up to $1.00. Sell and buy fees are included. Partial sells wait for fresh liquidity before a replacement buy; insufficient depth or cash can delay a recovery buy. Remaining shares hold to official resolution, with no TP or SL. Recovery resets at each new window and is not guaranteed. Freqtrade is prediction-only in dry mode; no live orders are sent.
