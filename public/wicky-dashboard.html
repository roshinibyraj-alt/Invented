Market-making rules
Passive quotes · controlled inventory · paper fills only
20-share clips
Fair valueBinance BTC model uses the two latest completed 5-minute candles and the current window’s live price.
QuotesPlace post-only simulated bids and inventory-backed asks for UP and DOWN, at least one tick from the opposing best price.
SpreadStart at a 2¢ half-spread and widen with volatility, up to an 8¢ half-spread.
InventoryQuote 20 shares at a time; cap unresolved inventory at 100 shares per outcome. Skew prices to reduce the heavier side.
Fill simulationA book touch alone never fills. Require a fresh CLOB trade print from the opposite taker side at or through the limit after estimated queue volume is consumed.
Window cutoffCancel resting quotes in the last 30 seconds. Remaining inventory waits for official resolution of its original market.
ExecutionPaper-only. No wallet or real order is connected; maker rebates shown are estimates.
Market-making P&L is not guaranteed: quotes can fill just before prices move against inventory, and fills may be partial. Public trade prints do not reveal your true queue position, so paper fills use a conservative queue buffer but remain estimates. Maker rebates are not guaranteed. Any inventory left at expiry is settled using the official Polymarket result.
