Recent closed trades · realized P&L

Paper-only BTC 5-minute strategy. At T−3 seconds, compare live BTC with the previous closed one-minute candle close using the final 57 seconds: higher means UP; equal or lower means DOWN. At window open, place one $0.45 limit buy for 500 shares on the signalled side. Use available ask fills at or below $0.45 at their better prices; if shares remain unfilled for one second, paper-fill the remainder at $0.45. Entry and take-profit limit fills have no fees; estimated maker rebates are credited to the paper account. Once filled, place a $0.99 limit sell, counted as $1 per share. No stop loss. Any shares without a TP fill by window end wait for official Polymarket resolution. Paper history resets on process restart.
