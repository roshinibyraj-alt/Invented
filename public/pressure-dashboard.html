The rules currently running in this paper bot
100 shares per signal
Initial ordersAt each window open, place resting buy limits for 100 UP shares and 100 DOWN shares, both at $0.30.
First fillA fresh best ask at or below $0.30 confirms the 100-share fill at that ask, capped at the $0.30 limit; then cancel the other side’s resting order.
Reversal buyIf the filled side’s midpoint reaches $0.20 or lower, immediately market-buy 200 shares on the opposite side. This triggers at most once per window; taker fees are modeled.
Hold to resolutionThere are no sells, take-profits, or stop-losses. Filled shares stay open until the official result of their original market settles them.
Unfilled limitsIf neither side fills, both limits remain resting until the window ends; then they are cancelled.
