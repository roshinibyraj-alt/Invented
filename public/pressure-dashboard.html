Strategy

Three-rung limit ladder, each side on its own

Rung 10.40TP 0.90
Rung 20.35TP 0.80
Rung 30.30TP 0.70
  1. BTC and ETH, UP and DOWN, each run an independent ladder of three 100-share limit bids.
  2. Fills are partial and only as deep as the live book supports at that price.
  3. Every filled share rests a take-profit sell at its rung's TP price.
  4. No rearming: each rung trades once per side per window. Fresh ladders start next window.
  5. No stop-loss. Shares still open at expiry wait for the official resolution.
  6. Maker fees are $0. Estimated rebates use each fill's price: shares × market fee rate × price × (1 − price) × assumed rebate rate. BTC rate 0.05; ETH rate 0.01. The maker rebate rate requires eligibility and may be overridden by a campaign. Estimates are not credited to equity.
