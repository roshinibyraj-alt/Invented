The rules currently running in this paper bot
100 shares per signal
MeasureUse the last five seconds of Binance aggressor notional and Polymarket CLOB trade flow, top-three-level depth imbalance, and microprice.
ConfirmBinance and Polymarket must point the same way; each must exceed 0.10 pressure and their combined score must reach ±0.30 for three one-second checks.
Independent buysEach newly confirmed signal sweeps visible asks for 100 shares on that same side. UP and DOWN may both stay open. Partial fills are allowed; remaining shares cancel immediately. Buys below a $0.05 best ask are blocked.
Profitable opposite exitDuring a signal trigger, buy the signalled side regardless of the opposite position. Also sell all opposite-side shares only when the fresh-bid estimate is net-profitable after fees; otherwise hold them.
Final take-profitA fresh best bid at $0.99 closes that side at $1 per share. This is the final TP, separate from profitable signal-triggered exits.
Stop and settleA fresh best bid at $0.01 or lower closes that side at $0. At window end, any remaining shares wait for that market’s official result.
CostsMarket buys and signal-triggered sells include modeled taker fees; the final limit TP may earn an estimated rebate.
